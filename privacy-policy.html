Privacy Policy for Cursor Overlay

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Cursor Overlay ("the app") is a desktop application for Windows that draws a customizable ring and click animations around your mouse cursor, for use in screen recording, teaching, streaming, and presentations. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and, more importantly, what it does not do with it.

Summary

Cursor Overlay does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections. Everything the app does happens locally on your own computer.

Information the app accesses

  • Mouse position and mouse button events. To draw the overlay in the right place and animate clicks, the app reads the current position of your mouse cursor and left/right mouse button presses in real time, using a standard Windows system input hook. This information is used only to render the on-screen visuals as they happen. It is never recorded, saved to disk, or sent anywhere, and it is discarded immediately.
  • The app does not capture keyboard input. Cursor Overlay only reads mouse movement and mouse button events. It does not capture, record, log, or transmit keystrokes or any other keyboard input.

Information stored on your device

Your visual preferences and saved presets (colors, sizes, effect styles, and similar settings) are stored locally in a settings.json file inside your own Windows user data folder. This file never leaves your computer and is not shared with the developer or any third party. Removing the app removes your ability to use these settings; you can also delete the file yourself at any time.

Data sharing

None. The app does not sell, share, or disclose any information to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party, because it does not collect or transmit any information in the first place.

Network use

Cursor Overlay does not connect to the internet and does not send or receive any data over a network.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
